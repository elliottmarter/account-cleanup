Preparing your export

The app ignores passwords, but you shouldn't have to take its word for it. It's recommended to export as CSV and remove the passwords yourself, so none are ever uploaded:

  1. In Bitwarden (web vault or desktop app) choose Tools (or File) → Export vault and pick .csv — not .json or the encrypted option.
  2. Open the file in Excel, LibreOffice Calc or a text editor and delete the login_password and login_totp columns. You can also delete notes and fields, which may contain secrets too. The app only needs name, login_uri and login_username.
  3. Save it, still as CSV, and upload it above.
  4. Afterwards, permanently delete both the original and the edited export (and empty the recycle bin).

Other password managers work too (Chrome, Firefox, 1Password, LastPass, KeePass): keep the name, URL and username columns and delete the rest. A Bitwarden .json export is also accepted, but it still contains your passwords when uploaded.
